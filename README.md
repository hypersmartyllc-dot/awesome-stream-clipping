# Awesome Stream Clipping [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of tools and workflows for turning long Twitch, Kick and YouTube streams (and podcasts) into short vertical clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

> Maintained by the team behind [OptimaClip](https://optimaclip.com) — so we're listed too, but we try to keep this list neutral and useful. PRs welcome, including for competitors.

## AI clipping tools (stream / VOD)

- [OptimaClip](https://optimaclip.com) — AI clipping agent: takes a Twitch VOD, Kick VOD, YouTube link or upload, finds highlight moments with a virality score and a plain-English "why this clip", captions and reframes to 9:16, and schedules posts to 8 platforms. EDL/SRT export for Premiere, DaVinci and Final Cut. [Twitch](https://optimaclip.com/twitch-clip-maker) · [Kick](https://optimaclip.com/kick-clip-maker) · [Gaming](https://optimaclip.com/gaming-clip-maker)
- [FluidCut](https://fluidcut.app) - Captures YouTube Live, Twitch and Kick streams while they run (beta) and clips finished streams from an uploaded file or YouTube link into captioned vertical clips.
- [Eklipse](https://eklipse.gg) — AI highlight clipping aimed at gaming streamers.
- [StreamLadder](https://streamladder.com) — Converts Twitch clips to vertical formats with layout templates.
- [Powder](https://powder.gg) — AI gaming highlight detection.

## AI clipping tools (podcasts and talking-head video)

- [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro) — AI clipping for long-form talking video.
- [Vizard](https://vizard.ai) — AI clips and editing for long-form video.
- [Klap](https://klap.app) — Turns long videos into short vertical clips.
- [Munch](https://www.getmunch.com) — AI clip extraction with marketing-oriented insights.

## Captions and editing

- [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co) — Animated captions for short-form video.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) — Text-based audio and video editing.
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com) — Free video editor with auto captions.

## Free and built-in options

- [Twitch Clips](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/how-to-use-clips) — Clip up to 60 seconds from live streams and VODs.
- [Kick clips](https://help.kick.com/en/articles/7120566-how-to-create-clips-on-kick) — Kick's built-in clip tool.
- [OBS Studio Replay Buffer](https://obsproject.com) — Save the last N seconds of your stream with a hotkey.
- [OptimaClip free tools](https://optimaclip.com/tools) — Safe-zone checker for TikTok/Reels/Shorts, aspect ratio calculator, SRT to VTT converter (no signup).

## Open source

- [auto-editor](https://github.com/WyattBlue/auto-editor) — CLI that cuts silence and dead air from video automatically.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) — The underlying tool for cutting, cropping to 9:16 and burning in captions.
- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) — Open-source speech recognition, commonly used to generate captions.

## How to choose

| If you mainly... | Look for |
|---|---|
| Stream games on Twitch or Kick | Direct VOD import, game-aware highlight detection, vertical facecam + gameplay layouts |
| Record podcasts or interviews | Speaker-aware reframing, accurate captions, topic search |
| Run clips for clients | Team workspaces, brand templates, bulk scheduling |
| Want to finish edits yourself | EDL/XML export to Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut |
| Have no budget | Built-in Twitch/Kick clips + OBS Replay Buffer + CapCut |
